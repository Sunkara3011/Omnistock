Business Use Case: The company Omni-Stock Retail requires a multi-warehouse inventory control platform to track physical stock levels and streamline order fulfillment workflows across regional distribution hubs. Omni-Stock needs to update available stock counts automatically upon order placement, enforce low-stock replenishment alerts, and allow warehouse managers to update product catalogs. The company requires independent microservices for product cataloging, stock tracking, and order placement to maintain transactional consistency. Omni-Stock specifies JWT authentication, service registration with Eureka, API Gateway request management, and load balancing to ensure accurate stock reconciliation across frequent inventory updates.

Managers should: Monitor inventory levels, Manage product entries, The system must handle frequent updates and ensure data consistency.
